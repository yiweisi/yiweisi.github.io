# Teaching

## As the Sole Instructor (Oxford)

<hr>

**Linguistics – German Linguistics** (Hilary Term 2025)

<p><strong>Description:</strong> This course introduced second-year undergraduates to the grammar of German, focusing on argument structure and word order. Students explored empirical data from German and completed weekly problem sets, leading to an in-class presentation and final essay.</p>

<p><strong>Responsibilities:</strong> Course design, 6 weekly tutorials, supervision of final papers, and setting reading tasks and exercises in line with Oxford’s Faculty of Linguistics expectations.</p>
